# Components from bb's registry

`components/ui`, `lib` and `hooks` are bb's plugin registry items, copied
unchanged from `packages/plugin-registry/r/*.json` at the tag
`desktop-v0.49.0` of https://github.com/get-bb/bb (MIT, Copyright (c) bb
contributors). Items: button, input, textarea, label, empty-state,
dropdown-menu, confirm-delete-dialog and their registry dependencies.
Refresh them from the tag of the pinned bb release; do not edit them here.
