1ma's sidebar: bb's own thread list with a bots block above the projects.

## What you get

- Exactly bb's thread list: pinned threads, custom sections, projects, machines, nested threads, row actions and drag and drop.
- With the 1ma Bots plugin installed, a **Bots** block above the projects: each bot with its face, role, work status and number of conversations.
- Click a bot to open its first conversation; the arrow shows all its conversations as regular rows, and **Done: N** shows the finished ones.
- **+** on a bot opens the regular new conversation composer for that bot; **+** in the header opens the Bots page to create a bot.
- A bot's conversations appear only under the bot. A hidden bot's conversations stay in their projects.

## How it works

Choose **1ma Sidebar** as the thread list under Settings → Appearance. The first time it loads, it copies the layout preferences of bb's built-in thread list. Without the 1ma Bots plugin, with **Show bots** turned off, or while bots cannot be read, the list is exactly bb's.

## Settings

- **Show bots**: on by default.
